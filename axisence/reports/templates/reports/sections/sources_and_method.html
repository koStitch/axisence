Definitions

  • Licensed capacity is the maximum number of children a regulator allows a provider to serve. It is not enrollment and not availability.
  • Distances are straight lines from the target location, not walking or driving routes.
  • A ZIP Code Tabulation Area is the Census Bureau's approximation of a zip code area; it is not the zip code itself.
  • Census margins of error are at 90% confidence.
  • Prices are as stated by each provider, for the provider's own age groups and schedules. One provider's age groups and schedules are not assumed to match another's.
  • Monthly equivalent: a monthly price as stated; a weekly price × 52 ÷ 12; a daily price × the schedule's days per week × 52 ÷ 12; an hourly price × the schedule's hours per day × days per week × 52 ÷ 12, when the schedule states its hours; an annual price ÷ 12. The result is rounded to the nearest dollar, halves up, and is approximate.
  • Tuition comparison: each cell holds the monthly equivalents of every current price of the provider's own age groups that contain the age, from the group's youngest to its oldest age, both included, on schedules of 5 days per week.
  • Contact methods. Call: a researcher's telephone call to the provider. Website: a researcher's reading of the provider's own website. Email: a researcher's email exchange with the provider.

Transit access is not covered by this report.
